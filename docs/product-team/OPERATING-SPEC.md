# Codeweb direct workflow

Effective September 30, 2026. [TERMINAL-CONTRACT.md](TERMINAL-CONTRACT.md) governs current operation. The former Paperclip cycle spec is preserved unchanged in [history](history/paperclip-2026-09-30/OPERATING-SPEC.md).

## Objective

Turn evidence into bounded product improvements and user experiments through direct Codex execution. Keep the work understandable, traceable and resumable without a running task server.

## Work record

For an active initiative, record the user/job, intended outcome, evidence and counterevidence IDs, selected decision, spec and acceptance criteria, owner, dependencies, execution state, checks/results and next action. A small assignment can use the existing backlog entry; a substantial change belongs in its own linked spec. Do not create another queue engine or make document maintenance the objective.

Preserve the chain: source -> finding -> hypothesis -> decision -> spec/experiment -> task -> result -> revised decision. Use existing research IDs. Product and growth work should share outcome evidence while retaining separate tests for local value, recurring use and paid service value.

## Completion

Implementation completion requires relevant verification and a reviewable change. Learning completion requires an actual result or explicit inconclusive outcome, plus its effect on the decision. Keep unknown customer outcomes unknown. Do not infer product success from issue counts, passing technical checks or agent agreement.

Update CURRENT.md and the relevant work record after meaningful results or a handoff. Preserve historical evidence. If the task depends on unanswered user/participant input, record the missing fact and continue independent authorized work.

## Review

Use the review required by the change, including a separate author/reviewer when appropriate and authorized. Check the final files or commit; a material revision invalidates an earlier approval. Preserve charter and protected-harness requirements. The direct workflow is the user's selected operating approach, not a measured superiority claim.
