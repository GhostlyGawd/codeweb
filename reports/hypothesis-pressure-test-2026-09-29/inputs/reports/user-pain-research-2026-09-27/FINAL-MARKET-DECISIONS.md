## Falsifiable experiments — proposals only

- **Free brief:** once separately authorized, use consenting ordinary developers' real changes in a counterbalanced comparison against their existing grep/LSP/tests. Predeclare a useful outcome: at least two additional actionable missed-consumer findings in ten changes without increasing median review time. Reject the brief if duplicate findings or reading burden cancel the benefit. Measure voluntary reuse after two weeks; successful installation is not retention.
- **Shared cross-repo evidence:** with a consenting team, compare a hosted evidence packet to CI that checks out both repos plus their current PR template. Require named budget owner and recurring operational burden before testing price. Falsify if existing checks catch the same issues with comparable effort or no one needs the shared history. A later paid pilot must explicitly sell hosting, multi-repo aggregation or human attention; no local feature paywall.
- **Receipt:** compare source-linked change/validation history against ordinary PR attachments during a later regression investigation. Require faster correct reconstruction across multiple reviewers, then an actual independently authorized purchase/renewal decision. Likes, generated reports and stated interest do not pass.
- **Private boundary:** use synthetic repositories to test permitted A–B and B–C sharing while forbidding A–C transitive leakage, deletion and source revocation. A real security owner must approve architecture before private-source access. Reject hosted fit if policy prohibits required transfer. This report authorizes none of that execution or outreach.
