Diagnosis: We have a working local product and preserved problem research, but no observed evidence that Codeweb improves an ordinary maintainer's decisions enough to earn recurring use or a separate paid purchase.

Updated October 2, 2026. Owner: the active Codex session. Status: verified native CLI/source/release delivery complete; actual field work and customer effects remain pending. ## Outcome and user

Help an agent-heavy maintainer make a correct, consequential inspection or reuse decision with acceptable total effort on an actual change. Observe whether they choose Codeweb again when a later eligible task occurs. This is the working product hypothesis, not an established customer outcome.

The provisional segment is an individual maintainer working in unfamiliar or changing code who recently had unresolved affected-consumer or reuse uncertainty. Screen by a recent task and current workaround, not by job title or agent enthusiasm. Familiar native-success tasks, same-file references and intentional duplication remain meaningful controls. Ordinary maintainers, adjacent tool builders, reviewers and budget buyers are distinct roles.

Keep two free local jobs separate: **consumer-scope inspection** and **reuse-candidate discovery**. Codeweb can own assembling current, inspectable source facts and recovering when that collection fails. The maintainer retains architecture, semantic suitability, intent, behavioral validation and merge decisions. A compact brief is a delivery option to test, not the entire value proposition.

## Decision gates

**Technical readiness:** applicable NED cases pass on the exact candidate and claimed host surface, or unavailable/unsupported cases are explicitly excluded. A source-only check or installed plugin is insufficient for a native-availability claim. Unsafe empty/unknown/green implications prevent an unqualified trial-readiness result.

**Local pilot:** preserve the provisional gate of at least two additional correct actionable findings across ten changes with no higher median combined inspection/review effort. Equal-time correct additions count. Faster incorrect answers fail. Pure equal-quality efficiency is recorded separately; it does not pass that gate. Adjudicate severe misses and unwanted edits individually; a median cannot erase harm. A shortfall is reported, not filled with artificial tasks.

**Recurring value:** record later eligible opportunities and actual optional choices, including learning that removes the need for continued use. Opportunity frequency, task value and use are separate. The pilot supplies directional evidence, not product-market fit.

**Paid value:** adequate CI gets a real chance to finish the job. Count setup, maintenance, triage, policy and runner recovery against actual recurring service benefit; exclude free-local and ordinary-CI savings. Buyer authority, payment and renewal remain distinct observations.

## Keeping the system current

At a meaningful result or handoff, update the selected task, append its evidence and decision change, reconcile CURRENT/roadmap, and regenerate the readable register if its authoritative JSON changed. Preserve prior versions in Git and immutable source packets. Review active priorities weekly when the work is active; this is a recommended session cadence, not an installed recurring job.

Refresh only the affected evidence when the selected host, source, package, persona, alternative or price changes, a new counterexample appears, or the next action depends on stale facts. Record the review date, source identity and reason. Older evidence is historical context, not automatically false. Read [SCORECARD.md](SCORECARD.md) for definitions and the result/handoff fields.

Completion of a planning/build task is separate from user-benefit evidence. Unknown customer counts remain unknown; downloads and board task counts are not users or outcomes. Paperclip remains retired. Direct Codex is the user's operating choice, not a proven comparative performance claim.
