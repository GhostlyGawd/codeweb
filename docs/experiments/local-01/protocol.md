# LOCAL-01: frozen design rules for controlled source learning

Protocol `E01-2026-10-01-v1`; prepared October 1, 2026. This is an E-01 preparation deliverable, awaiting separate review. No screened, consenting, enrolled or measured participants exist in this packet. The design rules are frozen before observation; actual allocation, named adjudicator, consent, contact routes and participant setup remain pending. The entry checklist is `READY-CHECK.json`.

The original LOCAL-01 success rule remains **at least two additional correct actionable findings across ten actual changes, with no higher median total combined inspection/review effort in the Codeweb condition than competent native controls**. This small bounded pilot informs the next learning decision. It is not PMF, a population effect, a causal estimate, retention proof or permission to expand the product. A delivered protocol is separate from observed task value.

## Jobs and eligible ordinary work

**Scope:** an additional correct supported relationship or consumer changes a maintainer's inspection plan. **Reuse:** a source-compared candidate correctly changes an implementation plan beyond competent native search and normal duplication tools. Reuse suitability remains a maintainer decision. Analyze the jobs separately, even when reporting the pooled frozen gate.

A primary participant is an ordinary maintainer using their usual Codex setup, authorized to observe the agreed source, with a recent concrete unresolved episode and two upcoming distinct comparable changes in one job. Tool builders, reviewers and buyers can supply labeled auxiliary feedback; they do not silently replace the ordinary-maintainer cohort. Unresolved uncertainty, not disliking native tools, determines eligibility. Native-success, same-file references, intentional duplication, runtime/generated dependencies, no relevant match and later familiarity are retained where naturally available. Do not manufacture eligible work or a missed dependency.

Before outputs, record each change's source identity, requested behavior, scope/reuse job, prior familiarity, expected inspection difficulty (participant's low/medium/high judgment), approximate affected files, available tests and source authority. The participant and assigned independent adjudicator agree that the pair has comparable inspection decisions; document differences, including task size. Matching limits remain visible. Neither the oracle nor matching is an exhaustive dependency ground truth.

## Allocation rule, frozen now; people assigned later

Aim for five people with two distinct comparable real changes each: ten changes, five native and five treatment. Provisional slots are S01/S02/S03 for scope and R01/R02 for reuse. Slot orders are fixed: S01 native→treatment; S02 treatment→native; S03 native→treatment; R01 treatment→native; R02 native→treatment. This produces three native-first and two treatment-first pairs; five pairs cannot balance first order perfectly. Report order and possible learning/contamination effects.

Qualify by actual screening and consent timestamp; break equal timestamps using ascending anonymous participant ID. Assign the earliest eligible, consenting participant with two comparable changes to the first unfilled slot of their naturally available job. Freeze pair task IDs and allocation before any condition outcome is observed. Within a pair the participant chooses the natural chronological order of the two changes before seeing output; apply that slot's predefined condition order. No person repeats the same change for an independent timing comparison, including a replay on a reset worktree. Global `change_id` values must be distinct.

The recorded chronology must support this rule: screening completion ≤ qualification ≤ final trial consent < actual allocation freeze < condition task start. Confirm adjudicator consent and availability before allocation. After allocation, record the actual case source pin before freezing its oracle; the oracle explicitly references that same private source-identity record. Verify each condition's actual configuration before starting its task. Consent and allocation precede Codeweb setup start, and setup confirmation precedes verified treatment configuration/task start. Native-first participants may complete their native case before their later Codeweb setup; the helper does not require introducing treatment during a native-first control.

If actual available tasks cannot fill 3 scope/2 reuse, the preregistered fallback is to fill remaining slots from the other job in qualification order, preserving the vacant slot's condition order. Record the actual mix and availability-based reason and freeze it before any condition measurement. The five-person/ten-change gate and two-findings/effort thresholds do not change. A missing subgroup cannot receive a job-specific success claim. If fewer than five actual consenting people with ten eligible distinct changes are available, report an incomplete pilot. Do not replace a case after seeing its result or exclude failures to complete ten successful cases. Record withdrawals and access failures, and retain the original allocation and replacements as deviations; a new complete confirmatory cohort would need its own pre-outcome freeze.

## Independent adjudication and oracle

Before counted measurement, appoint an actual named human with confirmed availability, consent and permission to see the agreed source. Store their identity privately behind an A-series ID. This person must not be the participant's decision maker for that case. Disclose other conflicts, including a Codeweb author or facilitator role; if independent judgment cannot be established, do not score a pass. A generated persona, the author agent's self-review or another same-author judgment is not human independence.

The method `source_case_adjudication-v1` is frozen: participant confirms task boundaries/authority and relevant acceptance constraints; adjudicator independently inspects the pinned source and relevant contracts/tests, records a bounded oracle with exclusions, and signs it before the condition starts. Hold exact undiscovered reference/candidate contents back from the participant until after their recorded decision to avoid coaching. The oracle may include unknown runtime/semantic dependencies. After the case, compare each claimed addition, missed relationship, unwanted edit/abstraction and final decision with source and checks. Obtain separate participant and adjudicator judgments and keep disagreement. New evidence can amend the oracle with a dated reason and original preserved, but unresolved material uncertainty blocks the gate; do not revise correctness to favor Codeweb.

Adjudication is complete only when source validity, final decision correctness, finding correctness/actionability, harm severity and material unknowns have explicit agreed judgments. A source reference alone does not establish usefulness. Missing/unavailable adjudication remains awaiting adjudication and cannot pass.

`decision_maker_actor_id` must be this case's enrolled `participant_id`, with participant effort under that same actor ID. The appointed `adjudicator_id` is also the adjudicator's effort actor ID and cannot alias any enrolled participant. Human identity/independence still needs private operator confirmation; separate strings do not authenticate separate humans. Missing task source pins, source/oracle pin mismatch, missing owners or contradictory chronology cannot score a pass.

## Conditions and real session procedure

The native condition preserves useful grep/search, LSP, source access, ordinary agent instructions, tests, clone detection, PR/CI and expert help, with Codeweb tools/hooks/instructions inactive. Treatment preserves the same host/model and useful native workflow and adds only the frozen Codeweb source MCP treatment. Do not suppress helpful participant configuration or force calls to claim ordinary usability.

P-04 ignored user config and disabled apps/hooks in synthetic fixtures. Those fixture controls are not an ordinary participant baseline. Use participant-approved per-session configurations that reproduce their useful controls in both conditions. If useful controls or permission policy cannot be maintained inside the approved treatment scope, record a setup limitation and defer the case. Do not broaden approval by inference. A changed host/model/client/parser or tool beyond the eight requires its own readiness evidence before counted use.

Before each condition, freeze a minimal reviewed configuration inventory and observation method; do not export personal config. Verify native absence honestly through effective frontend/session inspection, inactive Codeweb skill/hooks/server, and no Codeweb process/calls. Server nonlaunch alone does not prove all frontend definitions absent. In treatment, observe actual initialization/call/result and source inspection through a participant-reviewed minimal event note. Merely installing, listing tools, automatic cards or prescribed calls is exposure/setup, not activation. An inaccessible or contaminated control/treatment is retained and blocks a clean comparison.

For each actual change: record ordinary native inspection and its competent plan; treatment then records the decision immediately before Codeweb evidence, adds the approved workflow only if relevant, and captures the changed or unchanged decision. Do not coach a preferred answer. Open source for references/candidates; continue normal editing/review/tests. Observe null findings, dismissals, errors, permissions, recovery, normal checks and facilitator assistance. Record contamination or cross-task learning. The participant may decline a call, a candidate, an abstraction or the trial. Useful decisions that require no Codeweb addition remain native successes.

An additional finding must be source-backed, correct, relevant, actionable, beyond the recorded competent pre-Codeweb stage, separately agreed by participant and adjudicator, and linked to the actual changed decision. De-duplicate the same relationship/candidate/action within a case. Two references for one unchanged conclusion are not automatically two findings. This definition does not claim native tools would never find it later. A previously missed relationship in a competent native case is scored as a native finding/miss, not as a hypothetical treatment addition. All native and treatment findings remain in raw counts.

## Effort unit and frozen gate calculation

Primary effort is **human person-seconds**, converted to minutes for display: sum exclusive active work plus blocked waiting for each actor across setup, mapping/refresh, permissions/recovery, source reading, inspection, author, reviewer, normal checks, facilitation and adjudication. Include real participant, reviewer and facilitator work even when it rescues the session. One actor can occupy only one category/mode at a time; blocked wait ends when that actor performs other useful work. Concurrent work by two people adds two person-seconds because it uses two people's effort. Never add the same person's reading time to both author and source-reading categories.

Effort rows use unique effort IDs, actor IDs, UTC start/end timestamps, case attribution, phase and active/blocked mode. Cross-case overlap for the same actor is invalid. A category may be zero only with an explicit reviewed not-applicable/no-work reason; missing is null, never zero. Observed elapsed task time, request-to-useful-decision time, model wait, available model tokens and stdout bytes are separate fields. Model wait is not added again if already counted as a person's blocked wait. Overlapping model calls do not justify adding overlapping wall-clock time. Subscription coverage is not evidence of zero compute cost; unavailable token/fee fields remain unknown and cannot support token/money savings claims.

Count one-time Codeweb setup fully in the first treatment case where incurred and report it separately as well. Shared participant qualification/consent or pair preparation effort is recorded once against the first chronological case with a reason; setup unique to a condition is charged to that condition. Capture pre-outcome effort even if incurred before allocation. Cohort-only logistics unrelated to an individual decision are retained in `cohort_effort` and added to the reported pilot total; they are not hidden as free work. Keep per-case, condition, pair and cohort totals visible. No costs are amortized over assumed future frequency. Missing required task-work effort prevents a pass; missing optional monetary/token observations are disclosed, not invented. No timing of a repeated change is treated as another independent task.

For the complete ten-case cohort, compute the ordinary sample median of the five full treatment case totals and the five full native case totals. The effort criterion is `median(treatment) <= median(native)` with no tolerance, rounding benefit or outlier removal; calculations use seconds, display rounding comes afterward. Also show all five treatment-minus-native pair differences, job subgroups, first-order groups, all raw finding/miss/harm counts and actor/phase breakdowns. A favorable pooled median does not erase a harmful or expensive pair. Subgroup medians with tiny counts are descriptions, not inferential estimates.

The provisional gate can be marked pass only when all ten allocated cases are real, distinct, complete, consented, in the approved verified configuration, with confirmed independent adjudication and complete valid task-work costs; there are at least two agreed additional correct actionable treatment findings; the treatment median is no higher; final changed decisions are correct; and no severe harm occurred and no unresolved material harm/unknown remains. Any severe miss, unwanted edit or inappropriate abstraction blocks an unqualified pass even after mitigation; retain outcome and remediation. Incorrect acted-on guidance or an incorrect final decision cannot earn a pass by being faster. Unacted-on incorrect/noisy candidates are retained, judged and reported; they are not additional correct findings.

Fewer than ten valid cases, unavailable adjudication or missing costs gives incomplete/inconclusive, never pass. Once validity is complete, fewer than two additions or higher median effort fails this provisional gate. Pure equal-quality time savings are a separate descriptive efficiency outcome and do not satisfy the additions rule. Equal-time useful correct additions count. A result may justify retain/revise/stop per job while recurrence stays unobserved. State exact uncertainty and counterexamples beside any decision; do not move the threshold after measurement.

## Activation, follow-up and acquisition without telemetry

Activation requires a consenting qualified person completing an actual source-backed useful inspection/reuse decision. Setup completion, first invocation, calls, notices and automatic delivery are separate. Later use within the consented two-week window is optional and must have an actual later eligible opportunity. No invented task or additional reminder schedule is created.

Report later opportunity statuses explicitly: eligible optional useful use, eligible nonuse, eligible prompted/automatic use, no eligible opportunity, inaccessible and unknown. Opportunity-based useful-return denominator is enrolled consenting people with a confirmed later eligible opportunity; numerator is those with at least one optional useful use on such an opportunity. Eligible access failures stay in that denominator with an access label. Prompted/automatic use does not enter its numerator. No-opportunity and unknown stay outside the known-eligible denominator and remain visible; unanswered follow-up is unknown, not zero retention. Empty data produces unobserved return with numerator/denominator 0/0 and no percentage. Missing/no follow-up consent means not observed, not a new contact permission.

Self-reported discovery source is voluntary directional attribution. Record actual qualified conversation→consent→setup→useful task→later eligible use counts from consented offline notes with exact denominators. Leads, profile visits, invitations, downloads and stars do not establish users. An actual conversation may contradict the lead's public episode; retain the contradiction.

No product telemetry is needed to learn consented episode pain, observed decisions, correctness, total effort, setup friction or optional later use. These records cannot reveal the whole installed user population, passive retention, unobserved opportunities or general demand. Existing `scripts/lib/stats.mjs` keeps local counter/date receipts beside the graph; `CODEWEB_NO_STATS=1` disables writes, races/fail-open behavior can undercount, and automatic delivery/calls are not useful decisions. `regressionsFlagged` is not bugs avoided; `cardCallersFollowed` is an edited-file correlation, not proof of reading or causation. Week/session timestamps increment on `briefInjected`; Codex MCP-only sessions with hooks disabled do not supply a valid return proxy. Sibling pending-card data can contain symbol/file names and is sensitive. Never request full stats, pending cards, graphs, history, source or raw transcript dumps. Optional participant-reviewed selected aggregate counts with explicit separate consent may contextualize exposure; no such sharing is required for this gate, and no collection or transmission is automated. Public npm/download/star/fork counts describe acquisition surfaces; they do not identify people, useful tasks or retention.

## Data and publication boundary

Use the offline JSON/CSV templates and `summarize.py` only on explicitly selected records. Keep actual participant ledgers, private source, identity/contact crosswalk, raw notes and recordings outside the public repository. Local study IDs are research labels, not product accounts. Store only agreed data, with a private agreed retention/withdrawal date and permitted access people. Prefer minimal source locators and reviewed decision summaries; source remains on participant machines unless separately consented. No credentials are collected.

Observation, recording, follow-up route, optional aggregates and public case/quote publication have separate consent choices; a public profile, public issue or anonymization is not consent. Default publication permission is false. Publication requires both the exact selected derivative's participant approval and a repository artifact allowlist/privacy scan by the owner. Small subgroup counts can identify people; prefer suppressing identifiers/quotes and getting exact clearance. Helper output is an internal aggregate, not automatic publication approval. Do not publish this private folder wholesale.

Design amendments preserve the old version/hash and date/reason, and freeze before subsequent condition measurement. New outcome evidence is appended with source IDs; it does not rewrite the frozen research. Send the exact frozen file manifest to a separate critic. Author verification does not self-approve research quality.
