Keep this as resolved counterevidence, not an evergreen pain claim. CW-T015 explicitly limits missing transcript observations to 2.1.251.

## Audit change and experiment

Change to the audit interpretation: a visible in-agent brief is not enough; prove availability, invocation and receipt before judging its content. This is an evidence overlay, not a new visual inspection or modification of the baseline.

Proposed, not executed: in a separately authorized comparison, give native LSP/search/tests and Codeweb the same supported-language change, measure actual call, delivery timing, consumer recall, output size and user correction. A native result that is equally complete and cheaper rejects the incremental-value claim. This does not authorize the charter-gated parked A/B work.

Unknowns: current cross-surface reproduction, retained use, real review time, and whether users want another integration. No theme reached two empty targeted sweeps; no saturation asserted.
