These formulations can score an equal-time useful discovery differently. Both syntheses already preserve controls, adverse outcomes and unexecuted-test status, so this is a bounded editorial correction rather than a finding of conflicting customer results.

**Required disposition:** State separately how decision quality, severe misses/unwanted edits and total effort govern the provisional final decision. Explain the A/B wording choice. **Acceptance:** A reader can apply the rule consistently to those cases without inferring measured results. Withdrawal and compounding tests remain separately proposed stronger tests, not silently added obligations inside the original five-person/ten-change/two-week sprint.
