# Lens 12 — Ceiling of the hypothesis

Issue: COD-91 (`b095ce67-4c3e-4a89-8733-391b8527ce07`); run: `d4f5357a-1928-4b1d-bc5d-f9e5164b1be6`. Input manifest SHA256: `748f176be9d48ccf383ff6a6a601b56e7a8337bc898d27c2f1c319462a7e9852`.

Verdict: **revise the overall strategy; narrow the local bet; leave the distinct paid job unresolved; reject an automatic free-to-paid progression.** A perfect six-line brief can earn value by removing a recurring discovery step. It cannot by itself take over choosing abstractions, validating behavior, or coordinating other people. The packet does not show that its bounded step is substantial enough for lasting reliance, nor that its beneficiaries will buy the separate hosted job. This is a limit on the evidence and causal argument, not proof that demand is absent.

The stronger rival interpretation is that Codeweb can remain a useful free inspection aid while native tools, tests, existing PR artifacts and occasional human judgment own the rest. A narrow tool need not become a platform to be worthwhile. Conversely, an infrequent consequential task can matter; daily usage is not a prerequisite. The discriminating question is which recurring work actually returns when the tool is removed.

| Candidate | Potential recurring work removed | Remaining burden / capability ceiling | Buyer implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conditional brief | Discover relevant existing code and mapped consumers | Suitability, design choice, behavior and unmapped edges remain | Free local job; no necessary buyer |
| Context reconstruction | Reassemble revision-bound source facts across sessions | Intent, rejected approaches and pending task state are not a source graph | Free locally; no demonstrated memory moat |
| Supported change evidence | Assemble deltas and locate documented checks for review | Human understanding and real behavioral checks remain; free gate already delivers structural evidence | Generic receipt is weak paid value |
| Managed coordination | Schedule/rerun agreed cross-repo checks and assemble states | Explicit relationships, access, runners, check semantics and recovery must be supplied; current proposal is unbuilt | Plausible only where adequate CI leaves recurring work and a buyer |

These are bounded alternatives, not a roadmap or authorization to build. The commercial ceiling cannot be quantified from this packet: there is no validated revenue target, market size, buyer cohort or service margin.

### L12-F05 — reject

The automatic progression from useful free brief to durable commercial business is unsupported. Retain the free hypothesis for testing and reject that progression as the present investment rationale.

**Actor/task and conditions:** Local developer advocate and separately identified team purchaser; these roles must not be merged. Commercial ambition means recurring purchase and renewal, not a specified revenue target: the packet supplies no validated market size, retained cohort or unit economics.

**Strongest case:** CW-Y001 reports paid review of complex PRs despite low frequency, showing that infrequent consequential work can still be valued. Thus low invocation frequency alone cannot disqualify the free aid or paid services.

**Strongest countercase:** CW-Y001 purchases adjacent AI review, not Codeweb coordination, and does not establish observed renewal or final spending authority. CW-Y007 has no buying intent; native/control successes persist. Founder brief explicitly leaves users and return use unknown.

**Failure scenario:** A small pilot finds two useful pointers and satisfies its provisional gate; the team interprets this as permission to build a hosted business even though repeat use, residual paid work and a purchaser remain absent.

**Weakest assumption:** The people benefiting from free discovery will encounter and buy the distinct hosted job in sufficient recurring volume.

**Local implication:** A useful free tool is an acceptable outcome. Passing the proof sprint establishes only a reason for further bounded reliance testing, not necessity or a broad market.

**Paid implication:** Split commercial validation from local validation. The packet cannot establish either a low or high revenue ceiling numerically. Do not rescue the thesis by inventing a platform or by monetizing the free job.

**Minimal falsification test (proposed, not executed):** Before any commercial commitment, require separate evidence records for later voluntary local use and a named buyer’s residual recurring team work after CI.
