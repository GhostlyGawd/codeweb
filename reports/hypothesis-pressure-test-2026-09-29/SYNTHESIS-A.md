## Why the strongest alternative may win

The strongest rival is **capable native search/LSP, small changes, existing duplication checks, tests, maintained instructions and ordinary PR/CI evidence**, with human judgment handling the remaining ambiguity. This rival explains both successful changes and why a correct structural list might add no value. In [CW-X001](https://www.agentconnect.md/blog/grep-beat-lsp-harness/), semantic navigation improved precision in a small builder experiment but did not improve overall caller recall; native grep remained strong and clean-code work incurred overhead. Source-bearing output helped the semantic path, but that is not a Codeweb outcome or proof of superiority. Original sections: “The agents route by task”, “Noisy codebases”, “Output shape”, limitations; capture `sources/phase-B-nonauthor/CW-X001.txt`.

[CW-Z006](https://www.florian.bruniaux.com/blog/articles/claude-is-my-second-contributor/) strengthens the rival: reported large consolidation followed human identification of patterns, and some duplication was intentional. [CW-Z001](https://shan-verse.com/blog/codex-after-the-update/) supplies a bounded native-success case. These do not disprove a narrower discovery aid; they prevent attributing the host's capability expansion to Codeweb. The test must hold host/model, available source and expert help constant. Faster lookup and ability to undertake a previously avoided task are separate outcomes.

The paid rival is especially strong. [CW-Y007](https://forum.cursor.com/t/any-way-of-using-multiple-repositories-in-bugbot/152120) reports repeated manual cross-repo checking, but the same author accepts CI/monorepo as more appropriate and has not investigated CI. An additional visible participant echoes interest in the captured thread; this is neither a separate established buyer nor permission to multiply the canonical incident count. Historical Bugbot restrictions are not current product claims.

The paid hypothesis therefore begins *after* the best feasible ordinary CI workflow. Its candidate job is dispatching agreed checks for explicit producer/consumer revisions, tracking pending/failed/skipped/completed states, rerunning when revisions change, recovering runner failures and delivering evidence to the right owner. If a simple checkout/type-check workflow does that adequately, hosted coordination has no demonstrated incremental job for that team.

## What Codeweb can explain, and what it cannot

The frozen code supports bounded source-fact collection: mapped relationships, source windows, deterministic similarity candidates and task-owned historical evidence. It can plausibly shorten an inspection path. It does not explain away agent noncompliance, absent human intent, poor architecture, wrong test oracles or broad review load. It cannot establish runtime completeness or semantic equivalence from a map or shingle score.

Three concrete limits must remain visible in any subsequent trial or edited recommendation:

- `inputs/scripts/find-similar.mjs` skips missing bodies, applies a body cap and a similarity threshold, yet its plain-text empty-result branch says “looks novel; safe to write.” This is unsupported by that computation. L04-F02 is carried forward as a concrete claim problem, not fixed in this read-only assignment.
- `inputs/hooks/pre-edit-impact.mjs` selects a highest-incoming-edge symbol, truncates callers and returns silently on several unavailable/no-signal paths. A low-fan-in edited symbol may be missed. Explicit receipt diagnostics do not prove equivalent ambient presentation. A short card and a one-second warm target remain proposed experience constraints, not observed behavior.
- Refresh-only checks can leave duplication unevaluated; hook and CI-gate checks differ. Test-neighbor heuristics, recorded coverage hits, structural green and actual behavioral success need separate provenance. A test can pass with a bad oracle. Preserve the original baseline through refresh/repair and expose absent pre-edit evidence as unknown.

A complete **factual collection** job can finish while behavioral validation remains unfinished and assigned. “Own the decision” should be revised to “assemble inspectable facts for the declared decision.” Human design and merge responsibility remain. The service version has a different completeness bar: the recipient must get usable revision-bound evidence without repeating the same coordination and reconstruction work.

## Reliance, memory and the ceiling

No lens establishes Codeweb return, withdrawal loss, team dependence, payment or renewal. Twelve analyses of overlapping sources create no new customers. A first successful discovery could teach the maintainer enough that removal causes no later loss; that is durable value, not failure. Conversely, removal could improve the workflow by eliminating redundant reads and noise. Both outcomes must remain admissible.

Count eligible opportunities rather than days or automatic calls. At follow-up distinguish no relevant task, access failure, eligible nonuse, prompted use, passive exposure, deliberately retained automation and useful voluntary action. Low-frequency consequential work may be valuable; no eligible task during a short window is inconclusive. Do not extend outreach or manufacture tasks to satisfy a quota.

Existing task-owned receipts preserve historical witnesses; they are not automatic project memory. The frozen store checks task/root identity and has finite storage with deliberate cleanup. Seamless relocation, semantic exception invalidation and source-policy-safe sharing are not established. A saved architecture rationale can become wrong without an identity change. Compounding requires net reconstruction avoided after capture, freshness, retrieval, curation and correction—not growth in stored bytes. No proprietary-data moat follows.

Reviewer evidence is another conditional direction, not a default expansion. [CW-Y012](https://www.beyondautocomplete.nl/ai-writes-faster-than-we-can-review-heres-how-we-fixed-that/) describes insufficient summaries and existing artifacts; “Solutions”, “Artifacts”, “Where we are now” are captured in `sources/phase-B-nonauthor/CW-Y012.txt`. 1. **Operability:** under a separately authorized proof sprint, pin source/package and actual intended host/worktree. Demonstrate an ordinary request, real source links before editing, correct target selection, uncertainty, expansion, baseline-preserving recovery and uninstall. 3. **Later reliance:** after useful first work, observe naturally occurring later eligible choices. A matched, counterbalanced with/without comparison after familiarization is needed for withdrawal claims; retain learned knowledge and exports. Two initial tasks alone cannot settle this. If no opportunity occurs, leave recurrence unresolved.
4. **Separate paid qualification:** reconstruct a team's recent producer/consumer checks with its actual budget and source-policy owners. First inspect/cost adequate CI. Only a recurring residual job warrants a separately authorized bounded service test. Net service value subtracts setup amortization, ongoing maintenance, triage, runner recovery and policy work, and excludes free-local savings. Payment and later renewal need their own observations.

The sprint's proposed two additional findings across ten changes with no higher median effort is a provisional small-sample decision aid. It must not conceal severe misses, unwanted abstractions or reliance on reminders, and cannot prove prevalence or business viability. No new trial, quota, commercial offer or charter pricing decision is authorized here.
