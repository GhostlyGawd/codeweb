# Private screening, consent and session scripts

Drafts only. No sends or conversations occurred. Use only the actual selected recipient, sender, route, timing and content once authorized. First obtain agreement to a problem conversation; do not imply a public thread is a recruitment route. Keep contact identity in the private crosswalk. Do not promise faster edits, complete coverage, safe reuse or free compute.

## Problem conversation: seven episode questions, before any demo

1. Walk me through the latest change where deciding what to inspect, or whether code already existed, mattered. What were you trying to change?
2. What did you do to investigate it, in order, and which agent, search, LSP, tests or people helped?
3. What did that investigation tell you, and what did you decide? What was already resolved?
4. What remained uncertain, if anything? How did you handle it?
5. What work or waiting did it take you and anyone reviewing the change? Which parts can you recall or check, and which are unknown?
6. What similar work is actually upcoming, and what source can you authorize us to observe? It is fine if none is planned.
7. Where do you normally hear about tools you try, and how do you decide whether to use them again?

Ask neutral clarification of their answer without adding hypothetical harms or naming a feature as the expected solution. Capture participant-reviewed minimal notes. An answer that native tools were sufficient is a retained result. After question seven, close the discovery segment and record eligibility; do not retroactively reinterpret answers after a demo.

Screening fields are facts to verify, not extra episode questions: maintainer versus builder/reviewer/buyer role, ordinary Codex host/client/model, recent unresolved scope/reuse decision, source authority, two distinct comparable changes, useful native controls and acceptable observation/data boundaries. Mark unknowns honestly. Source readiness and permission/frontend gaps may make a willing person ineligible for this scoped run; willingness does not establish usability.

## Participant invitation/consent explanation, after discovery

“We are studying whether a local repository map helps with actual inspection or reuse decisions beyond your normal search and checks. The candidate is an unreleased source build tested only in a controlled Codex MCP setup with eight preapproved tools. Default permissions, product skill, global lifecycle and Claude use are outside this trial. The software runs locally and we will not add product telemetry or an account. Your normal coding provider still operates under your usual account; we do not collect its credentials or change that agreement.

“If you choose to participate, we would compare two distinct comparable changes you already plan, one with your normal tools and one adding Codeweb. We will capture all setup, waiting, inspection, review/check and assistance effort, including failures. An independent human would review only the source and decisions you permit. Findings may be useful, unhelpful or incorrect; you decide your edits and keep normal validation. You may decline a tool call, pause, stop or withdraw. No purchase is expected. You do not need to provide full source, graphs, stats, history or a raw transcript.”

Agree the named people with data access, exact source boundaries, minimal notes to keep, deletion/retention date and withdrawal route before observing. No recording by default. Record explicit separate yes/no answers for observation, installation/per-session permission changes, named adjudicator source access, recording, later follow-up route/window, optional reviewed aggregate counts, exact anonymized case/quote publication and any compensation (none approved at preparation). Do not bundle optional choices with participation.

Read back choices and obtain explicit consent through the participant's authorized route. Store consent timestamp and private evidence reference, never credentials. Declined publication means no participant-specific public case or quote; discovery/follow-up permission is not publication permission. A study opt-out does not change the product's free availability. Before each source session, confirm consent still applies and allow stopping.

## Separate optional demo/trial segment

Label the transition: “The problem conversation is complete. Here is the limited workflow we are studying.” Explain graph limits, source opening, normal checks and the exact eight-tool permission choice. Do not demonstrate the participant's hidden oracle/task or coach expected findings. If a demo influences the task, record that exposure and potential learning effect. A synthetic demo can show mechanics; it does not become a real task or a usability success. Invite the participant to choose whether to proceed with their real work.

At the competent pre-Codeweb checkpoint: “Please record what you would inspect and do based on your normal tools so far, including what is already resolved.” After treatment evidence: “What, if anything, changes in that plan? What source supports that decision? What would you dismiss?” This captures changed and unchanged decisions without requiring an addition. Keep source reading and facilitator assistance timed.

## Adjudicator appointment and scoring script

Secure an actual named human, role/conflict declaration, confirmed availability, source access consent and consent to keep their judgments before counted measurement. “You will independently inspect the authorized pinned source and acceptance constraints, record a bounded oracle before the task and withhold undiscovered findings from the participant. Afterward, please judge relevance, actionability, correctness, final decisions, misses and harms separately from timing. Please record unknowns and disagreement; an unresolved case cannot pass.”

The participant separately confirms source/task boundaries and their action. The adjudicator signs each source judgment and harm verdict. Do not substitute another agent, a self-review or an absent person for this role. The helper verifies explicit fields; it cannot verify the authenticity or independence of a human appointment.

## Optional follow-up, consented route within two weeks

One permitted follow-up only within the agreed route/window; no reminder schedule is installed. Ask whether any actual later eligible scope/reuse work occurred. If yes, record the actual task and whether use was optional, prompted/automatic, inaccessible or declined; ask what decision changed and what source/check supports usefulness. If no opportunity, record no opportunity. If no reply or unclear, record unknown. Record when familiarity or saved learning removed the need for another query. Do not create an opportunity or turn nonresponse into zero retention.

For optional manual exposure counts, request only the fields the participant reviews and explicitly agrees to share: for example a rounded count of useful invocations in the agreed window. They can instead report no counts. Never request full `stats.json`, pending-card symbol/file data, history, graphs, source or raw transcripts. These counts cannot replace decision/adjudication records.
