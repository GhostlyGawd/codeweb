# First cohort and growth plan

Diagnosis: Distribution should first produce qualified learning about actual Codeweb task value; we do not yet know which segment returns or which channel produces that outcome.

Prepared September 30, 2026. Owner: Codex. Work: G-01/G-02/G-03. Sources: F-C01/F-C05/F-C09/F-C11 and their findings. The plan uses the charter's free local product and no-telemetry boundary. It does not introduce accounts, a community platform or paid ads.

## Now: qualified conversations and a real-task cohort

Start with people describing recent unresolved reference/reuse decisions and the one preserved hands-on Codeweb report. Screen with a recent episode: what changed, what they inspected, what their agent/native tools did, what they missed or already solved, what the workaround cost, and whether a similar task is upcoming. Ask where they discover tools; channel selection follows those answers.

[PROSPECTS.md](PROSPECTS.md) holds five source-grounded leads and one separate team-discovery lead with role/fit uncertainties. They are not six enrolled users. Three ordinary/unknown-role sources and a native-success control complement the prior user; adjacent-tool builders from the earlier kit remain optional expert feedback, not substitutes for ordinary maintainers.

G-01 rechecks each source/profile, contact route, role and any previous message before preparing the exact draft and sender. G-02 sends only the approved recipient/channel/content or a bounded standing policy. Consent, replies, declines and nonresponse are recorded privately. Agents handle this work; the founder does not need to supply a contact list. No invitations have been sent as part of this planning packet.

The existing issue #92 maintainer follow-up already asks for update confirmation. The September 30 readback has no reporter reply. Avoid stacking a duplicate technical request; any distinct research invitation needs its own authorized timing/route. Third-party issue threads and public profiles do not imply permission to recruit there.

After an actual conversation, invite suitable consenting people to [LOCAL-01](../experiments/codeweb-local-value-pilot.md). Keep problem discovery separate from demo/trial feedback. Target five participants/ten actual changes provisionally; participation shortfalls remain real evidence boundaries.

## Activation

Observe installation, host discovery, first source-backed useful decision and recovery with the user's ordinary workflow. Record facilitator assistance and clean docs-only first use separately. A successful install or MCP query is not first value. Improve the exact friction observed; the [NED spec](../specs/native-evidence-delivery.md) supplies technical readiness.

Preserve existing user-selected homepage work. WEB-01 reconciles the actual selected candidate before new copy/layout changes. Describe demonstrated tasks and limits in users' terms; new benefits need results and normal review before becoming public copy.

## Next: one channel with an explicit thesis

After task benefit and voluntary return justify wider acquisition, G-03 selects one place the qualified audience already uses. Candidate communities, GitHub issue discovery, educational content or referrals are possibilities; none is currently declared a proven channel. Use conversations and actual source attribution to choose one bounded experiment.

Record how people heard about Codeweb voluntarily, the originating channel, qualified conversations, trial consent, setup, first useful decision and later eligible use. Distinguish source attribution from causality. Set the experiment's effort/time boundary before execution. Wider first-50 work follows a repeatable route to users receiving value, rather than a count of downloads or stars.

Keep invitations individual and problem-specific. A launch, newsletter, community or paid campaign receives a separate concrete scope; no distribution action is created by this document.

## Separate buyer discovery

The preserved multi-repo champion is a lead for B-01, not a buyer. Find the accountable check owner and policy owner, identify budget authority, and inspect two actual change cycles plus adequate CI. If recurring chasing/rerun/reconciliation remains, B-02 measures net costs and prepares a real offer test within the charter's hosted boundary.

Teams is already permitted by the charter; the old downloads trigger is not restored. Local adoption is not a buying obligation. Approximately EUR10/active-author/month remains intent until service and buyer evidence support an actual offer. Payment, renewal, support expectations and service delivery cost each need observations.

## Completion and freshness

Update the selected growth task, private contact/consent record, sanitized result and roadmap after meaningful replies or outcomes. The scorecard keeps zero newly enrolled trial participants distinct from unknown existing user count. Refresh an active candidate/channel when the source, profile, consent or workflow changes. Do not rescan the whole market as a substitute for the next actual conversation.
